Music Strategy Rules

6 principles derived from 52 posts of audio performance data.
Rule 1: Match music energy to process
CNC = AC/DC, Metallica, Dire Straits. Sanding/finishing = Electronic chill or Lo-fi. People/culture = Pop or hip hop. Energy match matters more than genre popularity.
Rule 2: Hip hop works broadly
Kid Cudi (viral), Baby Bash (10 saves on digital twin), Outkast (6,291 views) all delivered. Choose songs that match the pacing of the clip, not just what's trending.
Rule 3: Do not chase trending audio without context
The viral post worked because the song matched the story: working while the world sleeps. Fit beats trend. A trending sound that doesn't connect to the content will underperform.
Rule 4: Original audio is not a fallback
Bubble blasting (7,223 views), CNC capacity (6,580 views), and guide coat all performed well on original audio. If the process sound is satisfying, keep it. Shop sounds are on-brand.
Rule 5: Classic rock is on-brand
Dire Straits (8,975 views), Fleetwood Mac (4,692 views), AC/DC (two posts). Classic rock resonates with the likely buyer: 35-55 year old marine industry professionals. Lean into it.
Rule 6: Avoid random pop hits with no thematic connection
The music should reinforce the mood, not distract from the work. A catchy song that has nothing to do with precision manufacturing pulls the viewer out of the content.

Hashtag Rules

ProtoTech MFG Instagram strategy, established Jun 30, 2026.
Rule 1: Max 5 hashtags per post. Never exceed.
Rule 2: Audience type is the primary filter, not post count. A tag with 30K posts reaching naval architects beats a tag with 30M posts reaching lifestyle consumers.
Rule 3: Mandatory B2B set: #moldmaking + #boatbuilder + one from: #navalarchitecture / #compositetooling / #marinecomposites.
Rule 4: CNC posts: add #cnc and/or #cncmachining.
Rule 5: Defense posts: #defensetech #usv #autonomous allowed. Keep 5-tag limit.
Rule 6: Introduce one new hashtag per post maximum. Rotate candidates in one at a time so performance can be attributed to a specific tag. If it underperforms across 3 posts, retire it.

What Is Working

Key signals from 52 posts. Source: INSIGHTS tab, Jul 2026.
CNC Time Lapse = Highest Reach Content
The viral post (191K views, post #39) was a night CNC time lapse. CNC Install lapse (#36) also outperformed. Time lapses compress impressive work into shareable moments. Run a time lapse on every major CNC job. This is repeatable.
Defense/USV = Highest Quality Lead Generator
Post #48 (Jul 9 defense/USV): 167 likes, 14 saves, 14 new follows from 4,082 reach = 5.17% engagement. Highest engagement in the dataset, excluding viral. Defense contractors and program managers are watching this account. Post MORE defense capability content. Use #defensetech and #usv.
People and Culture Posts Drive the Most New Followers
Post #43 (Luke approves, ship it!): 31 new follows. Post #46 (boat reveal): 33 new follows. Post #32 (bubble blasting): 23 new follows. People want to see your team. Faces, reactions, and energy convert viewers to followers better than process content alone.
Saves Are Your Best Lead Quality Signal
Post #34 (digital twin): 10 saves, 4.82% save rate. Post #39 (viral): 94 saves. Post #48 (defense): 14 saves. Saves mean "I want to come back to this" which = someone considering your services. Track saves per post as your primary lead-gen metric, not likes.
Full Story Content Is Your Best Client Pitch
Post #30 (full design cycle, concept to CNC): 8,975 views, 23 shares. Shares = "I am sending this to someone." This is what ProtoTech does that competitors like Michael Peters Yacht Design cannot. Make this a recurring content series.
Short Reels (8-13 sec) Are the Sweet Spot
Q2-Q3 2026 posts at 8-14 seconds consistently hit 3,000-7,000 views. Longer reels (20+ sec) from 2025 averaged under 2,500. The B2B audience does not want a tutorial. They want a moment: hook, payoff, done.

What Is Not Working

Gaps to fix based on performance data.
Sporadic Posting in 2025 Cost Momentum
22 posts in 9 months in 2025, including a 2-month gap August to October. Q2-Q3 2026 = 19 posts in 6 weeks. The algorithm rewards consistent activity. Keep the 3-posts-per-week cadence minimum.
Generic Hashtags Suppressed Reach on 20+ Posts
Tags like #ManufacturingExcellence (dead, 0 posts/hr), #Teamwork, and #BehindTheScenes do not reach boat builders or naval architects. The B2B hashtag strategy established Jun 30, 2026 is the correct approach. Apply it going forward.
Defense/USV Is Underrepresented (3 of 55 Posts)
Highest-engagement content category, also the least posted. ProtoTech has a defense program, a CAGE code (9NP50), and SAM.gov registration. Post about it: hull platform development, autonomy integration, defense engineering capability. Check with Dustin before publishing.
No Carousel Strategy in 16 Months
Only 3 carousels total, and the Alera reveal carousel scored the highest save rate in 2025. Carousels average 3x the reach of single images. Use for: before/after mold progression, design concept to render to physical, multi-step process explanations. These can be built from existing project photos.
Audio Energy Mismatch Suppressed Watch Time (Jul 23 Post)
Post #56 (Jul 23, "Nobody likes imperfections") used Kendrick Lamar's "TV Off" over methodical quality assurance content. Result: 1.9K views, approximately 5 seconds average watch time on an 11-second reel — under 50% completion. Low completion rate signals poor content quality to the algorithm and limits distribution. The mismatch between a high-aggression track and deliberate, slow-paced process work likely caused early scroll-off. Audio energy must match what is on screen. For finishing, sanding, and QA content: chill hip hop, lo-fi, or classic rock. Trending aggressive tracks do not fit this content category regardless of cultural relevance.

Content Team Guidance

Content priorities and production checklist based on performance data.
Priority Content Queue
1. CNC time lapse: any active overnight or extended run.
2. Mold or plug reveal: any piece coming off polished.
3. Team moment: reaction, delivery, approval.
4. Process explanation: what IS bubble blasting, what does guide coat do.
Caption Formula That Works
Short declarative hook (3-8 words). One sentence of context or a brief process description explaining what's happening in the video. Optional CTA. 5 B2B hashtags. Examples: "While the world sleeps, the CNC keeps cutting." / "Luke approves, ship it!" / "You don't rush a finish like this." / "Guide coat goes on first, it maps every imperfection so nothing gets missed." Short, confident, no filler, no emoji overload.
What Always Needs Approval Before Posting
Any mention of client name, project, or vessel. Any defense/USV content. Any price, timeline, or capability claim. Any merch or promo. Caption must be approved before posting.
